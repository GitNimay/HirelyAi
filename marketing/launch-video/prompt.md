# Launch video brief (made with /blank-page)

Draft: `a 10 sec launch video for hire excellence`

Mode: brief. The look comes from the app's own landing page: soft, grainy film-sky photos,
Newsreader serif headlines and the dark product UI. That is a fence, so no outside aesthetic is used.
The are.na and Pinterest searches could not run because this sandbox blocks those hosts, so the motion
is described in words below, marked `[? motion ref]`.

```
in stewdio, new file hire-excellence-launch, create the following, in film-sky editorial (grainy pastel long-exposure sky, newsreader serif, one dark ui card):

1. 0-2s: a paper resume (≥60% frame height) drops onto the a1 sky, its text lines writing in top to bottom; left of it, serif "resumes tell you / what people wrote."
2. ~2.2s, the turn: the resume's 24 text lines tip upright and slide into a row, becoming the bars of a live voice waveform, while the paper turns into the dark interview card in one continuous morph, not a cut [? motion ref: line-to-bar morph]
3. 2.8-4.5s: "5-min AI voice interview", red rec dot, timer ticking down from 05:00; the waveform talks; two transcript lines type in under it. headline: "hear how they think."
4. 4.6-6s: the bars fly into a ring, the ring draws to 87 and three criteria bars fill. headline: "get a ranked shortlist, with the reasons."
5. 6-8s: the card shrinks into row #1 of the applicants list, then the camera pulls back 1.5x→1x to reveal the full hire excellence app window, with rows 2-5 sliding in
6. 8.2-10s: the window falls away, the sky warms to a2 (violet-peach); logo, then "hire on signal, / *not on resumes.*" and the url. hold under 1s

very snappy, ease-in-out, one continuous motion; 16:9, 1920x1080, 30fps, the whole thing 10s

refs:
- a1 public/landing/hero-sky.webp: look, the sky ground for beats 1-5
- a2 public/landing/tile-profile.webp: palette, the warmed sky in beat 6

- newsreader + geist (the app's fonts): use as-is
```
